# Ruined Academy — combat prototype

Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7 and press **F6** with `main.tscn` open, or **F5** to run the project.

Move with **W A S D**. **Left-click** to fire one projectile toward the mouse. Each of the three stationary enemies has 3 health and disappears after 3 hits. A projectile deals 1 damage and disappears on impact or when it leaves the map.

The map is 1600 × 960 pixels with grass, earth paths, and a stone courtyard. The camera follows the wizard and stops at the map edges.

The larger Pixel Lab art batch is stored separately in `../art_library`. Only the wizard and three enemy sprites are used by this scene.
